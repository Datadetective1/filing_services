# Terms of Service: additions for business services (DRAFT)

**Draft for professional review. Not published.**

## Business services

Besides annual report filing, Filewell offers registered agent service, a registered agent and
annual report plan, and, in some states, help forming a business ("business services"). Each
is described on its page and in the order summary you see before paying.

## Our provider

We provide registered agent service through a wholesale provider, Registered Agents Inc.
Its name and address appear on the state's records as your registered agent. You deal with
Filewell for everything: orders, billing, support and the documents you receive. We may
change providers; if that requires a state filing, we will tell you first.

## What you agree to give us

You agree to give us accurate information about your business and a contact person we can
reach, including a physical address and phone number, and to keep them up to date. Our
provider may need them at short notice to meet legal requirements.

## Not legal advice

Filewell is not a law firm and does not give legal, tax or financial advice. If you receive a
lawsuit or any legal document, contact a lawyer.

## When service starts

Paying starts your order. Service starts only when the appointment is confirmed (and, when
you switch agents, when the state records the change). We will tell you when it is active.
