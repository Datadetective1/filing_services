**Draft for professional review. Not published.**

## What we collect for business services

- Your business's details (legal name, state, entity type and number).
- A contact person for the business: name, physical address and phone number.
- For a switch, the name of your current registered agent.
- Documents received for your business (service of process, government notices, mail), which
  may contain personal information about you or others.
- Billing details handled by our payment processor (we do not store card numbers).

## How we use and share it

- **Our registered agent provider** (Registered Agents Inc.) receives your business details and
  contact person so it can act as your agent and meet legal requirements, including requests
  that must be answered quickly. It is the agent of record on state filings.
- **The state** receives what its filings require (for example, the agent's name and address).
- **Our payment processor** handles payments and renewals.
- We keep documents in private storage, show them only to you and our staff, and record every
  download.
