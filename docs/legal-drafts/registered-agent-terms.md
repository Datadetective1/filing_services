# Registered agent service terms (DRAFT)

**Draft for professional review. Not published.**

1. **The service.** For each business and state you order, our provider acts as your
   registered agent: it receives service of process and official government notices at its
   address in that state. We add what it receives to your dashboard and email you. For
   service of process we also try to reach you by phone.
2. **No guaranteed delivery time.** We aim to tell you promptly, but we do not guarantee how
   quickly a document reaches you. Keep your email and phone number current and check your
   dashboard.
3. **Regular mail.** Legal documents and government notices are always included. Ordinary
   mail is scanned; a limited number of items is included each year and further items may
   carry a per-item charge, shown to you before you are charged. Junk mail is not scanned.
   Packages and verification mail from online services are not accepted. Physical copies are
   available on request only for a limited time.
4. **Switching agents.** If your business already has an agent, we file the change with the
   state. You pay the state's fee. Your service starts when the state's record shows the
   change.
5. **Your responsibilities.** Keep your business information and contact person up to date,
   read what we send you, and act on it in time. Do not set up mail forwarding from the agent's
   address. Do not use the agent's address for anything other than what the service allows.
6. **Ending the service.** You can turn off renewal at any time before the renewal date (see
   the subscription renewal terms). Before your service ends, you must name a new registered
   agent with the state. If we are still listed after service ends, our provider may file a
   resignation with the state, which can cause your business to fall out of good standing.
   After service ends, documents are no longer forwarded to you.
7. **Our right to end the service.** We may end the service for non-payment, misuse, false
   information, or if our provider ends its service. We will give you as much notice as we
   reasonably can and help you move to another agent.
