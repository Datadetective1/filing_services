# Subscription renewal terms (DRAFT)

**Draft for professional review. Not published.**

- **Automatic renewal.** Registered agent service and the registered agent + annual report
  plan renew automatically every year, for another year, at the same published price, charged
  to the payment method on file, until you turn off renewal.
- **What you see before paying.** The order summary shows the price, that it renews yearly,
  the renewal price and how to turn renewal off. You confirm that you understand before paying.
- **Reminders.** We email you before each renewal (currently 30 and 7 days ahead).
- **Turning off renewal.** You can turn off renewal online from your dashboard at any time
  before the renewal date. Your service continues until the end of the year you paid for.
- **Failed payments.** If a renewal payment fails, we tell you and try again. Your service is
  not ended because of a failed payment, but if you do not pay we may end it after notice, and
  our provider may resign as your agent.
- **State fees in the plan.** The plan's annual report state fee is never charged on the
  renewal date. We ask you to review and authorize each filing and charge the state fee then.
