# Legal drafts for business services

**Drafts for professional review. Not published, not legal advice, not in force.**

These drafts cover registered agent service, the registered agent + annual report bundle,
formation, subscription renewal and cancellation. They are written in plain language to match
the existing Filewell legal pages, and they describe the product as built in this branch.

Nothing here is wired into the site. The published legal pages (`/legal/*`) are unchanged.
A lawyer should review every draft before any of it is published, and the operating entity
named in them must match `NEXT_PUBLIC_LEGAL_ENTITY` at the time (see the Harborlune LLC
transition checklist in `docs/REGISTERED_AGENT.md`).

## Unresolved issues for the reviewer

1. **Operating entity.** Harborlune LLC is not formed. Who contracts with customers until it is?
2. **Automatic renewal laws.** Several states (for example California and New York) have
   specific auto-renewal disclosure, consent and cancellation rules. The checkout shows the
   renewal price and terms and requires a tick box, and customers can turn off renewal online.
   Confirm this meets each applicable law, including any required annual reminder wording.
3. **Agent of record.** Registered Agents Inc. is the agent of record on state filings; Filewell
   resells and supports. Confirm how this must be disclosed to customers and on state forms.
4. **Liability.** The provider caps its liability at fees paid in the prior 365 days and
   indemnifies only for gross negligence or worse (agreement as reviewed, unsigned). Filewell's
   own cap and exclusions need to be at least as protective and enforceable.
5. **Service of process timing.** The provider says documents are "typically" uploaded within
   24 hours, with no guarantee. The drafts promise no delivery time.
6. **Resignation risk.** After cancellation or non-payment, the provider may resign as agent,
   which can affect the customer's good standing. The drafts warn about this. Confirm the
   warning is sufficient and whether advance notice to the customer can be promised.
7. **Mail forwarding.** The provider requires clients not to set up USPS forwarding from its
   address. Confirm how to impose this on customers.
8. **Data sharing.** The provider may request client contact details (including for Wyoming
   law) with 12 hours to answer, and the provider's Exhibit A contemplates handing over client
   contact details for collection. The privacy draft discloses sharing with the provider;
   confirm the Exhibit A scenario is acceptable or negotiated out.
9. **Unauthorized practice of law.** Formation and change filings are prepared from customer
   answers. Confirm the scope language is enough in each state.
10. **Sales tax.** Whether registered agent or formation services are taxable where customers
    are located has not been researched.
11. **Price changes.** The drafts say renewals are at "the same published price"; decide
    whether and how a future price change is notified.
