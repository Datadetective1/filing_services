# Formation service terms (DRAFT)

**Draft for professional review. Not published. Formation is not offered yet.**

1. **The service.** We prepare and submit your business's formation filing to the state from
   the answers you give and confirm, and pay the state's filing fee on your behalf.
2. **Fees.** You pay our service fee and the state's filing fee, shown separately before you
   pay. The state's fee is passed through at cost.
3. **Registered agent.** Registered agent service is optional. We add it only if you choose it,
   and you may name any registered agent you like.
4. **Your answers.** You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you give,
   including names, addresses, owners and managers, and for choosing the right type of entity.
   We do not advise on entity choice, tax treatment or legal structure.
5. **The state decides.** The state may reject or delay a filing. If it is rejected, we fix
   what we can and ask you for anything else. We do not guarantee approval or timing.
6. **Refunds.** Before we submit, you can cancel for a full refund. After we submit, the state
   keeps its fee; we refund our service fee only if we made an error or could not complete the
   filing.
